LIGHT-PROVER

Sound verification of Lean 4 proofs with the official comparator's checks, measured faster on large proofs.

Light-Prover checks a project against a pinned challenge: the target statements and axioms are compared by the official comparator code, and every declaration they depend on is checked by the unmodified official Lean kernel sources and by an independent second kernel. The build and the export in front of that judge are optimized for time and memory and are not trusted. For proof developers, AI provers and RL pipelines, CI on proof repositories, and the final check of large proofs. Linux x86-64; not yet externally audited.

Made for real workflows

The same trusted judge behind every interface, from a single proof attempt to a 175,000-module proof.

Proof engineering

Sound verdicts on every change, and CONDITIONAL verdicts that name the open sorry lemmas while a proof is still being written.

AI provers and RL

A sound verdict per attempt over CLI, HTTP or MCP; attempts that compile but do not prove the pinned statement are rejected.

CI on proof repositories

A PR gate with SARIF output and replays of real development histories (experimental in v0.1).

Final sign-off

Verification of large proofs from sources on one server, or of an existing export within a 64 GB or 128 GB memory limit (24-32 threads) with a low-memory judge.

What is trusted

Only the judge is trusted: the pinned official challenge and comparator configuration, the official comparator code, the unmodified official Lean kernel sources (driven by Light-Prover's reviewed reader, parallel replay and wiring) and a mandatory independent second kernel. The fast build, the native exporter, the schedulers, caches and services are untrusted producers: a bug there can cost a verdict but cannot turn an invalid proof into ACCEPTED. Soundness model.

Get started

git clone <repository-url> Light-Prover && cd Light-Prover
python3 -I fastverify/tools/configure.py --elan-home "${ELAN_HOME:-$HOME/.elan}" --light-lean "$PWD"
fastverify/bin/fastverify ./my-repo ./challenge ./out --preset sound-auto --challenge-sha256 "$PIN" --comparator-sha256 "$CPIN"

$PIN and $CPIN are the sha256 of the official challenge export and of its comparator configuration; both are required, because the configuration decides which statements and axioms are checked. Building the pinned components and the first run: getting-started guide.
